# Planner job-first — TinySpec

## Context and authority

The owner reports that agents obsess over bookkeeping instead of advancing planner
stages. The owner selected TinySpec and approved the five-bullet task scope in this
session. Exact combined artifact approval and routing belong to
[run evidence](https://github.com/elsonidoq/skills/blob/5088b25877467887dd41c9547197c6da43b3c165/memory/coordination/evidence/2026-10-08-planner-job-first/SUMMARY.md).

## Scope and approach

Add a prominent job-first contract to `skills/planner/SKILL.md` and focused checks in
the existing planner checker/tests. Apply the contract to both planning paths and
stage briefs. Keep the operational owner in the skill, not duplicated guidance.

## Acceptance

- A1: The job is primary; bookkeeping must remain a small fraction of total effort.
- A2: Once required prerequisites are satisfied, advance the next authorized stage;
  do not reopen preparation without concrete invalidating evidence.
- A3: Reuse applicable settled evidence, keep required records terse, and prohibit
  duplicate records, repeated polishing and unchanged rechecks as substitutes for progress.
- A4: Preserve mandatory approvals, safety checks, permissions, reviews and commits;
  genuine blockers stop the affected stage with the smallest required resolution.
- A5: Focused positive and negative static checks detect missing job-first rules;
  existing planner checks still pass. Static success is not runtime-obedience proof.

## Bounds and validation

No numeric time quota, time tracker, dependency change, shared policy rewrite or live
provider exercise. No prototype: this is an instruction seam, inspected directly and
checked using the existing Python fixtures. External research is unnecessary because
no dependency/API behavior changes. Model time allocation remains unverified.
