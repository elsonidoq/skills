The runner owns configured command checks/reruns, focused checkpoint and independent review. Pending runner stages alone are not grounds for reporting blocked/failed/incomplete; workers still never stage or commit. Preserve any explicitly assigned worker-owned checks/manual evidence and semantic acceptance: a known failed or genuinely unrun worker-owned requirement is unsuccessful, not excused by green runner checks or permission. A reasoned correction awaiting the runner's promised rerun remains supported; it is not a waiver of that rerun or a mechanism for ignoring a worker's explicit unsuccessful return.
